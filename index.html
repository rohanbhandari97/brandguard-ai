YOUR BRAND PLAYBOOK

Simple rules. Consistent content.

These example guidelines are sent with each review request.

✦

Voice & tone

Be helpful, confident, empathetic, and customer-first.

AVOID“Buy now!” or “Hurry, act now!”
TRY INSTEAD“Explore a solution designed for your goals.”
!

Claims we can prove

Do not make absolute promises or market-ranking claims without approved evidence.

AVOID“No. 1 solution” · “100% results”
TRY INSTEADUse a specific, substantiated benefit.
◐

Accessible colors

Text should remain easy to read across backgrounds and devices.

STANDARD4.5:1 contrast for normal text
✓

Human approval

Automated checks help people review content. They do not publish content automatically.
